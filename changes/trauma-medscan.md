- Trauma Team MedScan: a paid MedScan tab in the Trauma Team app (20eb, once, through BangMe!). Call a remote specialist for a wound consult (100eb, +1 First Aid or Paramedic for an hour) or a surgical consult (500eb, +1 Medical Tech or Surgery for 4 hours); the +1 is a situational checkbox in the roll dialog and ends on world time. Plans can include MedScan (Executive does by default: consults cost nothing). The GM sets the prices, can answer each call themselves or have no specialist available, and gives or removes the app per character.
