- New GM window: the **Director** (the button beside the GM Phone's, a keybinding, or `api.director.open()`). A wide control view with tabs by GM task; Dashboard, Comms and the Garden are built, the rest come one at a time. It wears the Neon Agent look.
- **Dashboard:** the Agent Monitor itself (every control works), **Waiting on you** (players' contact requests, Great River orders, the Garden writer's draft batches) and the app switches and each character's signal.
- **Comms:** every conversation in one list with search; reply as the contact, as a group's NPC or as a service; New text to anyone, as anyone, caller ID optional. What you're typing survives tab switches.
  The GM Phone's controls are all there: whose phone, acting as, SIM tiles, the selected-token offer, pins, block, reveal, texts only, attachments and money.
- **Garden:** the GM Phone's Garden, wide: post and reply as any account, the feed beside the open post or Patch, and the Queue, Drafts, Accounts, Writer, Dating and Control pages.
- **Calls ring on the Director** while it's open (setting "Calls ring on the Director", on by default): Answer, Decline, Leave and End in a strip above every tab, no pop-ups.
- **Detach** moves the Director into its own browser window for a second monitor; close that window (or Dock) to bring it back.
