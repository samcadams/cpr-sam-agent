- CBK: Black Chrome's Night Market companion app (starts off; switch it on in Control). It searches for a database until the GM pushes GO LIVE on a Night Market; then players browse it like the book's catalogue (category tiles, search, "other categories", listing cards), tap BUY NOW!!! (charged at once through BangMe!, with a receipt) and pick the item up by showing the receipt at its booth while the market is live. Stock runs out live ("SOLD OUT").
- CBK on the GM Phone: make a market from any of Black Chrome's six Night Markets (A–F, with their booths and items matched to your compendiums) or a blank one, edit listings (booth, price, stock, hide, add any item), GO LIVE, and hand over or refund receipts.
